================================================================================
                    QUICK GUIDE: HOW TO CREATE A PROCUREMENT ORDER IN JIBE
================================================================================

This quick-reference guide outlines the standard steps to create, process, and 
submit a procurement requisition / order in the JiBe Marine ERP platform.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 0: USER ONBOARDING & IDENTITY INITIALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. When visiting the simulator, enter your name in the "What should we call you?"
   prompt dialog and click "Proceed".
2. The custom username and computed initials automatically populate across all
   screens, table "Created By" audit columns, and discussion feed cards (replacing
   "Ivan Xavier" and "IX").
3. User identity persists across sessions and pages independently of draft discards.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: INITIATE A DRAFT LIST IN LMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Navigate to: Top Menu > Procurement > Lists (LMS).
2. Click the "+ Create New" button in the upper-right corner.
3. Fill in the mandatory list header fields:
   - Vessel: Select your vessel (e.g., SESP Demo Vessel).
   - List Name: Enter a clear description (e.g., Chemicals - Deck & Engine).
   - Urgency: Set priority (e.g., D: Regular Spares/Stores, Emergency, Critical).
   - Vessel Department: Select the owning department (e.g., Engine, Deck).
4. Click "Create". 
   -> System generates a draft reference: LIST SPDM-60028/26 (Status: Active).
   -> Note: Navigating directly back to the landing page automatically discards
      in-progress draft data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: ADD METADATA & TECHNICAL CHECKLISTS (BUTTON STATE LIFECYCLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Initial Button States:
   - "Save List" button is DISABLED by default upon arrival.
   - "Create Requisition" button is DISABLED until items are added.
2. General Information:
   - Assign regulatory tags (e.g., MARPOL).
   - Enter internal operational remarks in the Remarks field (up to 2500 chars).
   -> "Save List" button becomes ENABLED as soon as edits are made.
3. Checklist Section:
   - Click "Edit", add checklist items, and save.
4. Attachments Section:
   - Click "Attach File", browse local file, enter remarks, and upload.
5. Click "Save List":
   - List commits to storage with green success toast.
   - "Save List" button REVERTS TO DISABLED state unless further edits are made.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: ADD LINE ITEMS FROM CATALOGUES & SEGMENT POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the left menu "Items" tab and click "Browse Item".
2. Select segment "Chemicals".
3. Browse catalogue categories and select items:
   - A&T Upgrades > EnviroLogic HF HP 32
   - Chemicals > Test UAT
   - Demo Catalogue > DCSI003
4. Click "Add to list" for each item (Quantity: 1).
5. Click "Back to List" in upper-left corner.
   -> Items reflect in list; "Create Requisition" button becomes ENABLED.
6. Segment Restriction Policy:
   - Incompatible segments cannot mix. Attempting to add Services items when
     Chemicals exist triggers the Segment Policy Error popup banner.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: CONSTRUCT & CONFIRM THE REQUISITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. From the top-right corner, click "Create Requisition".
2. Complete the requisition parameters:
   - Purchase Type: High Value Stores
   - Delivery Port: Pasir Panjang | Singapore (via searchable modal)
   - Delivery Date: 02-10-2026 (via October 2026 calendar popover)
   - Subject: "Chemicals for deck and eng"
3. Click "Save":
   - Confirmation dialog pops up: "Are you sure you want to create a requisition?"
4. Click "Confirm":
   - System generates Requisition SPDM-60037/26 with cyan confirmation dialog.
   - Status transitions to "Completed" on Page 3 and Page 2.
   - Discussion feed logs the creation under the active user's name and initials.
   - BOTH "Create Requisition" and "Save List" buttons permanently LOCK DISABLED.
